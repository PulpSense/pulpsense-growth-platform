# PulpSense pre-call email copy library

**Status:** Approved by Santi for implementation on 2026-08-11.

## Sequence rules

- Email 1 identifies Facebook/Instagram as the acquisition source. Later emails do not repeat it.
- Emails 2 through 17 each handle one objection or question.
- Appointment references are selective and varied. The full date and time are not repeated mechanically in every email.
- No nurture email contains the meeting join link. The separate Gmail reminder emails own meeting logistics and the join link.
- Case-study and proof-archive links are omitted until reviewed, real URLs exist.
- Each email ends with a casual sign-off from Santi.
- A standard business-address and pre-call opt-out footer will be appended automatically after the sign-off.
