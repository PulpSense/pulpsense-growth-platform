# Separate pre-call nurture from meeting reminders

Pre-call Nurture is a Trigger.dev workflow whose first message is delivered through Brevo's Transactional Email API seconds after booking verification. Trigger.dev owns its dynamic 4–18-message schedule, copy-module selection, idempotency, cancellation, and rescheduling. Meeting Reminders remain a separate Trigger.dev lane sent through the authenticated personal Gmail account at 24 hours, 2 hours, and 15 minutes before the appointment. Both lanes independently verify that the appointment is active and unchanged before delivery; nurture timing must avoid collisions with Gmail reminders.
